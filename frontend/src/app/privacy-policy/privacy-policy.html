2 What Information We Collect

2.1   Account & Authentication Information

You sign in with your shared Gavaller account (see §2 of the Gavaller Privacy Policy). Price Tracker uses from it:

  • Email address — to deliver price-drop notifications.
  • Username — shown in the interface.
  • Account ID — an internal identifier used to link your alerts to your account. Never shared externally.
  • Sign-in session — on the website, a secure cookie (§10); in the Extension, tokens kept in extension storage (§2.5). Never transmitted to third parties.
Matches Chrome Web Store declaration: Authentication information

2.2   Price Alert Data

When you create a price alert we store:

  • Product identifier — to know which product to monitor.
  • Target price or drop percentage — the condition you set for notification.
  • Alert status — whether the alert is active or paused.
  • Your account ID — to link the alert to your account.

The alert service also keeps a copy of your account ID and email address, refreshed daily from your account, so it can send notifications. This data is stored on our own server.

2.3   Email Notification Content

When a price alert is triggered, we send an email to the address registered on your account. The email contains the product name, old price, new price, and a link to the product. We do not store the content of outgoing emails beyond the delivery logs kept by our transactional email provider (Resend) for up to 30 days.

Matches Chrome Web Store declaration: Personal communications (emails)

2.4   Product Price Data (Scraped, Not Personal)

We collect publicly available product and price data from the webshops of the stores we track (currently Tesco Hungary and Auchan Hungary). This data includes product names, prices, and identifiers. It is not personal data and is not linked to any individual user.

2.5   Browser Extension Local Storage

The browser extension stores the following data exclusively on your device:

  • Language and theme preference
  • Product name cache — a small local cache used to display product names without extra network requests
  • Authentication tokens — used to keep you signed in, as described in §2.1 above

None of this data is transmitted to us or any third party, except that your login token is sent to our own API to authenticate your requests.

4 Server Logs and Security Monitoring

Requests to the website and the API pass through the Gavaller gateway, which records for each one: the time, the requested path, the response, your IP address, browser, country and city, your account ID if you are signed in, and for some features the search text, the product viewed or the settings of a price alert. These logs are kept for up to 180 days.

They are used to run and troubleshoot the Service and for the platform's security monitoring, which recognises crawlers, scrapers and attackers. A person reviews its results; nothing acts against anyone automatically. The logs are never used for advertising. Sections 3 and 4 of the Gavaller Privacy Policy describe both in full, including your right to object.
